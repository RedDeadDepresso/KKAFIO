# Organize Library

Opens one library window for sorting scenes, characters and coordinates into folders by hand, as tabs (**Scene**, **Character**, **Outfit**). Each tab keeps its own search, sort and selection, and a confirmed card is moved at once. CLI: `organize-library`.

The **Chara / Scene / Coordinate Directory** options default to the game's folders (the Scene tab is left out when Studio isn't installed), and **Use Cache** shares the Group tasks' caches.

## Using the library

The character library has a search bar, a sidebar with *All characters*, *Unknown series* (cards not in a series folder) and the series folders that exist, and a grid of covers with the character's name and its series — *Unknown series* until it has one. Click a card to select it (its border is highlighted); the arrow keys or **WASD** move between cards and **Enter** focuses the series input of the selected card. **Ctrl+click** (or **Ctrl+arrows** and **Space**) adds cards to the selection and **Ctrl+A** selects all; with several selected, **Enter** opens a small dialog with the series input, and Enter there moves all of them into that series folder. Or click that text directly to type a series for one card; suggestions come from the existing series folders and, when **Hints Language** is not `no`, from the anime, games, vtubers index. **Enter** (or clicking a suggestion) confirms: the card is moved into `<chara folder>/<series>/` at once, and the folder is created if needed. **Esc** cancels the input, **↑/↓** and **Tab** pick a suggestion. A name you pick from the suggestions is used as it is (the index's titles are what the suggestions offer); a name you type and confirm without picking keeps your spelling, except that it reuses the spelling of an existing folder of the same name (in any case), and is made safe as a folder name. Sort by updated date, name or size; the **Size** slider sets the cover size. A collapsible **Mod dependencies** sidebar on the right (the arrow strip on its edge collapses it) lists the mods used by the card under the cursor, missing ones first: a missing mod has a search icon that opens a Google search for its GUID, an installed one an icon that shows its zipmod in the file manager. It checks the game's mods folder, or **Installed Mods Directory** (`--installed-mods-dir`) and **Downloaded Mods Directory** (`--downloaded-mods-dir`) like Download Missing Mods does: a mod installed in either folder counts as installed (`--mods-dir` sets both). Cards marked in the library's **To Delete** list are handed to **Delete Cards** when the window closes (instead of going straight to the Recycle Bin): controlled by the **Delete Options** checkboxes (Check for Shared Mods, Include Coordinates; CLI: `--include-coordinates`, `--check-shared-mods`) — with **Include Coordinates** a character's matching coordinate cards go along, and with **Check Shared Mods** (`--check-shared-mods`) a zipmod is only deleted if no other installed card still uses it; zipmods are looked for, and deleted, in the **Downloaded Mods Directory**. Right-click a card (or press **F11**) and choose **Mark to archive** to move it to **To Archive**, an entry of the sidebar just above **To Delete**; right-click a card there and choose **Restore** (shortcut **Insert**, as in To Delete) to take it out again. When the window closes the **To Archive** cards are handed to **Archive Cards**, which bundles each one with its zipmods (and, for a character, its matching coordinates) into the **Archive Output Directory** (CLI: `--archive-output-dir`), in the **Archive Format** (7-Zip, ZIP or Copy; `--archive-format`) and with the **Archive Options**: **Combined Archive** (one archive for all the To Archive cards, otherwise one each; `--archive-combined`), **Include Sideloader Modpack** (`--archive-include-modpack`) and **Include Coordinates** (`--archive-include-coordinates`). The originals are kept. The library opens maximized and always includes subfolders (the **Include subfolders** option only applies to the LLM); folders starting with `_` or `.` (such as `_duplicates_`) are ignored. The sidebar can be walked with the keyboard too: click an entry, then **Up/Down** (or **W/S**) move through it; **Right**, **D** or **Tab** hand the keys back to the cards. From the cards, **Tab** or **Left/A** on a first-column card goes to the sidebar.
